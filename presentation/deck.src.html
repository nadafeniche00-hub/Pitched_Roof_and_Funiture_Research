Reading, 2024 to 2026

What the datasets and the new paper say

Dataset, 2025

ResPlan

17,000 flats as vectors and room graphs, in metres.

Its own checks became our rules: windows on a wall band 99.98 %, walls 10 to 40 cm 99.3 %.

arXiv:2508.14006 · v2, Aug 2026 · CC BY 4.0

The checks are used; the dataset itself is not loaded yet.

Dataset, 2024

MSD

5,372 plans of Swiss apartment buildings.

Imported: rooms, openings and door graphs, read safely, to calibrate sizes and neighbour rules.

arXiv:2407.10121 · ECCV 2024

No roofs and no heights in it: it cannot teach what is usable under a slope.

Paper, May 2026

Rewards you can verify

An LLM writes the plan as text; code checks it and rewards it: 0.15 against 2.5 for the diffusion baseline.

Lara et al. · arXiv:2605.14117 · Findings of ACL 2026

Its limits: no code compliance, nothing about roofs.

Same idea as the paper: a model proposes, code decides. Ours runs as a check, without training, and covers what their reward leaves out: heights under a roof, ergonomics, circulation.
